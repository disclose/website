---
title: "How do I interact with or contribute to the disclose.io projects?"
weight: 30
---

Glad you asked!

- <a href="https://policymaker.disclose.io" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Start a vulnerability disclosure program (VDP)</a>, or upgrade your VDP or bug bounty program to include best practices like Safe Harbor and proactive disclosure timelines
- <a href="https://community.disclose.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Join the community</a>, contribute or assist with vulnerability research, and help finders connect with security teams to alert them of identified risks
- Help us keep <a href="https://github.com/disclose/diodb" target="_blank" rel="noopener">"The Big List"</a> of known VDPs and bug bounty programs up-to-date by submitting a PR to the <a href="https://github.com/disclose/dioterms" target="_blank" rel="noopener">dioterms repo</a>
- Contribute to the <a href="https://github.com/disclose/dioterms" target="_blank" rel="noopener">dioterms open-source vulnerability disclosure policy</a> by <a href="https://github.com/disclose/dioterms/issues" target="_blank" rel="noopener">raising an issue on the repo</a>… or add a language or regional legal translation by <a href="https://github.com/disclose/dioterms/pulls" target="_blank" rel="noopener">submitting a PR</a>
- <a href="https://github.com/disclose" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Volunteer as a core contributor/maintainer</a> on one of our existing projects
- <a href="mailto:hello@disclose.io">Recommend a new project</a> to support our mission to make vulnerability disclosure safe, simple, and standardized.
