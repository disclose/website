---
title: "How do I interact with or contribute to the disclose.io projects?"
weight: 30
---

Glad you asked!

- <a href="https://policymaker.disclose.io" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Start or upgrade a vulnerability disclosure program (VDP)</a> with best practices like Safe Harbor and clear disclosure timelines.
- <a href="https://community.disclose.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Join the community</a> and help finders connect with the security teams who need to hear from them.
- Help keep the <a href="https://directory.disclose.io" target="_blank" rel="noopener">directory of VDPs and bug bounty programs</a> accurate and up to date.
- Improve the <a href="https://github.com/disclose/dioterms" target="_blank" rel="noopener">dioterms disclosure policy</a> by <a href="https://github.com/disclose/dioterms/issues" target="_blank" rel="noopener">raising an issue</a> or adding a translation via <a href="https://github.com/disclose/dioterms/pulls" target="_blank" rel="noopener">a pull request</a>.
- <a href="https://github.com/disclose" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Volunteer as a maintainer</a> on one of our projects.
- <a href="mailto:hello@disclose.io">Suggest a new project</a>.
