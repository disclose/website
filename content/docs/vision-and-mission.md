## Mission

> Make vulnerability disclosure **safe, simple, and standardized** for everyone. We're the open, vendor-neutral infrastructure that powers disclosure and security reporting.

## Vision

> Every organization welcomes good-faith security research under safe harbor, and no researcher risks legal harm for helping.

Our shorthand: a healthy **Internet Immune System**, or "Neighborhood Watch for the Internet."
