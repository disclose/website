How to read the edges

One convention runs through all four. It is the thing that makes the picture honest, and it came out of auditing the actual code behind each arrow.

solid: a machine does this dashed: a person does this the one relationship that makes disclose.io a commons

Variation 1: The flywheel

Loop first. The shape says "self-reinforcing system" before a single word is read. The hub sits at the centre as a multiplier on every edge, not as a sixth stage. Strongest claim, hardest to fit labels into.

Variation 2: The foundation

Dependency first. Everything rests on the standard. Reads as durable infrastructure, which is the language an enterprise legal or security leader already thinks in. Weakest at conveying circulation.

Variation 3: The transit map

Network first. Five lines, eleven stations, one interchange. Carries far more named properties legibly than a constellation can, and the interchange makes disclose.io's role obvious. Risk: the idiom is friendly, which may read as unserious to a general counsel.

Variation 4: The journey

Narrative first. Reads left to right as one sentence: a standard is set, a policy is drafted, a program is recorded, a contact is found, disclosure is coordinated, the result is measured and shared. Easiest to understand cold. The loop is the hardest thing to keep visible.
